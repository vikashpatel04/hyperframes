# 0 to 1: coming-soon film, three storylines

Source: https://0-to-1-waitlist.vercel.app (captured in `capture/`) plus the product brief. Every UI label below is from the site's own demo (Become a Software Engineer · 3 years · Beginner · 1 hour per day).

---

## A. "Zero, to one" ← recommended

**Why:** the logo _is_ the story. The mark is a dark capsule shaped like a **0** with "to 1" inside, so the film can literally fill a zero into a one.

- **Device:** the logo is the carrier. An empty capsule (the 0) opens the film, everything happens inside it, and it fills up as the plan comes together until it lands as the 0 to 1 mark.
- **Form:** one continuous take. The camera dives _into_ the zero, travels through the pain and the product, and pulls back out to the finished mark. There are no hard cuts.
- **First 2 seconds:** frame 0 is a huge, empty capsule outline on near-black, with **"Every big goal"** already landing beside it. On "starts at zero" the capsule is the zero.
- **Beats:** empty zero → the goal → 1,095 hours and nobody tells you what to do with the first one → the plan dies by Wednesday → "knowing what you want isn't knowing how" → the mark → goal / roadmap / today / adapt / assistant → the zero fills → Big goals. Clear next steps. → join the waitlist.
- **Look:** dark (zinc-950, the logo's own `#09090b`) for the struggle; it floods to **white shadcn** (zinc borders, 12 px radii, Inter) when the product arrives, so the film itself goes from dark to clear. One colour only: the ink-blue shimmer of the site's "Join the waitlist" button, kept for the final call to action.
- **Sound:** low, sparse pulse under the pain; it opens into a confident, steady groove on the reveal. Ticks on checkboxes, one soft impact as the zero becomes the one.

## B. "Sunday plan, Wednesday miss"

- **Device:** a moment in someone's week. **Form:** an object-to-object chain across one week calendar.
- A Mon–Sun week strip is the home stage. Sunday: an ambitious plan. Wednesday: one missed block, and the backlog piles up until Friday's column collapses. Then 0 to 1 rebuilds the _same_ week: the missed block slides forward and the week stays calm.
- **First 2 seconds:** "SUNDAY 9:47 PM" and a to-do list growing to 23 items.
- More relatable and less brand-led. The logo only appears at the end.

## C. "1,095 hours" (the bold one)

- **Device:** a number lands. **Form:** cut on the beat, type carries the argument, **no voiceover**, music only, about 30 s.
- "3 years." "1 hour a day." "**1,095 hours.**" "Nobody can plan 1,095 hours." "You can plan the next 25 minutes." Then the real Today screen and the mark, all cut to the music.
- **First 2 seconds:** "3 years." slams on beat 1, full frame.
- Punchy and great for Reels or Shorts, but it explains less about the product.

---

_(1,095 h = 3 years × 365 days × 1 hour, from the demo's own goal settings, and is labelled as an example.)_
