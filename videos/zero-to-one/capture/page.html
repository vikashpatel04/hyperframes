Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about 0 to 1, our planning principles, and the interactive demo.

0 to 1 is a goal-planning application designed to help you turn long-term ambitions into structured roadmaps and manageable daily actions.

You define a goal and your starting point, review a proposed roadmap, and use daily tasks to work toward your milestones.

The product is designed around your goal, timeline, and available time. A realistic plan should account for these constraints rather than assuming every deadline is achievable.

The intended experience includes reviewing missed work and adapting the schedule where possible, while preserving completed work and respecting your available time.

0 to 1 is being developed. Join the waitlist to express interest in early access.

The landing-page demo is a curated sample walkthrough that demonstrates how goals are structured into roadmaps, milestones, and daily tasks. It does not create a live account or require personal data.
