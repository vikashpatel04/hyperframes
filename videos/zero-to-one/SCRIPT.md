# 0 to 1: "The Note" (SCRIPT v3, for approval, nothing built yet)

**Length:** about 70 s · **16:9, 1920×1080** (9:16 cut later) · **Voice:** Kokoro `af_heart` (free, same as before)
**The story in one line:** a goal written on a sticky note three years ago, a day counter that keeps climbing, and the one thing that was always missing: _what to do today_.

**Two carriers run the whole film, and the camera never cuts away from them:**

1. **The sticky note.** It opens the film, peels off the monitor, becomes the goal you type into 0 to 1, rides the top of the roadmap, and goes back on the monitor at the end.
2. **The day counter** in the corner of the screen. It reads **Day 1,095** (three years of waiting) and resets at the end to **Day 1** (the first day of doing).

**Two worlds, one camera:**

- **Riya's desk:** warm, sunlit and textured. Cream wall, golden morning light, a wooden desk, a dark monitor, a yellow sticky note in real handwriting, soft depth of field. This is the opening frame: warm and inviting, not black.
- **Inside the monitor:** the product, in clean white shadcn with the orange accent. We enter it by pushing _into the screen_ and leave it by pulling back out to the desk. No hard cuts.

> She reads it every morning.
> And every morning, she asks the same question… okay, but what do I actually do today?

### What's different from the last version

- **The hook is a story with a mystery:** a note you can't read yet, and a counter racing to 1,095. The viewer waits to find out what it says and why it's still there.
- **The opening frame is warm and textured**, a sunlit desk, not black with a word.
- **One character and one arc:** the film starts and ends on the same shot, and the only change is the counter (_"Her Monday did."_).
- **Small details are planned per beat** (the "Small details" column), including the odometer counter with motion blur you asked for. Render-time motion blur will be on for every fast move.
- **Sound is planned per beat.** Beyond the bundled effects, I'll pull free CC0 sounds (room tone, birds, pen, paper, page flips, a rewind) from Openverse.
- **Assistant scene redesigned:** today's list visibly reflows at a readable size.

### Facts vs. story

- _Riya_, the 47 tabs, the 3 roadmaps and the 12% course are **story details**, not product claims.
- Every product step (goal → roadmap → today → adapt → assistant) is from your brief and the site's demo.
