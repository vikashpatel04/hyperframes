# 0 to 1: "The Note" (SCRIPT v4: deadpan hook · portrait for Instagram)

**Format:** **9:16, 1080×1920** (Instagram Reels) · **about 60 s** · narrator **Kokoro `af_heart`**, calm and deadpan (no acted emotion)
**Story:** Riya's three-year-old sticky note, a day counter stuck on **Day 1,095**, and the one missing thing: _what to do today_.
**Carriers:** the **sticky note** and the **Day counter**. Same desk shot at the start and the end; only the counter changes.

**Reels rules built in:**

- **Safe zones:** nothing important in the top 250 px (status and profile) or the bottom 380 px (caption and buttons). The right 140 px is kept clear near the bottom (like/share). Key text lives in **y 260–1500**.
- **Readable with the sound off:** every narrator line also lands as kinetic text in the **top text zone** (y 280–640), so it doubles as captions.
- **No silent gaps:** a quiet bed runs under everything (room tone, a clock tick, then music), and every visible action has its own small sound. "Freeze" moments use a **tape-stop** sound, never silence.

**Layout (portrait desk):** top text zone (y 280–640) · monitor with lock screen (y 640–1340, cut off at the right edge) · the note on its lower-left bezel · the desk and an out-of-focus plant fill the bottom (Instagram's overlay zone, decorative only).
