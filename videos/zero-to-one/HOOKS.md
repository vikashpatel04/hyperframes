# 0 to 1: sudden hook options (first ~4 seconds)

Each option replaces only the opening of Act 1 and then flows into the approved story. The note, the Day counter and "what do I actually do today?" all stay.

The narrator stays **af_heart**. Riya gets her **own reaction voice** (samples in `hook-samples/`), so the shock sounds like a person, not a narrator.

---

### My recommendation

**A (The Avalanche)** for maximum stop-the-scroll energy; it's the hook you described.

If you want the cleverest story, **C (The Memory)** plants "Monday" so the ending lands harder. A mix also works: **A's avalanche** with **C's memory card** as the last notification to fall.

### About the reaction voice

Free Kokoro voices read exclamations fairly flatly; they won't truly gasp. If you want a genuinely shocked "No WAY", HeyGen's free allowance (10 min/month with a sign-in) has more expressive voices. Listen to the samples first.
