# Counter Pay — Problem → Solution film (DRAFT v2, for review)

**Product:** [Counter Pay](https://github.com/vikashpatel04/Payment-QR-Standee-Enhanced): exact-amount UPI QR on a customer-facing screen, from one shortcut.
**Length:** ~75 s · **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080 (a 9:16 cut can follow) · **Voice:** Kokoro, local and free (`af_heart` or `am_adam`)
**Look:** the app's own palette: deep green-black night `#0c110f`, paper `#f3f5f2`, Counter Pay green `#14b37d`, loss red `#d93025` for the problem act. Inter, heavy weights for the kinetic words.

**Continuity idea (no camera breaks):** the **₹40** is the thread through the whole film. It falls off the bill in Act 1 and drops onto a growing pile of lost round-offs. The pile becomes a counter that climbs to ₹18,000. At the turn the 40 rises back up, and in Act 3 the full amount flies into a QR that can't be changed. The camera never cuts; it follows the money.
